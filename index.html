THE LOST BLESSINGS

3D Story-Driven Adventure

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys

INTERACT: E Key to speak or collect items

HEAL: Press M to eat a Modak and restore 1 Health


CAMERA CONTROLS

Press C to toggle between Camera Modes:

  • Classic: Top-down isometric view (Default). WASD moves relative to the map.
  • Action Follow: 3rd-person camera locked behind Mooshak. W moves forward into the screen, A/D turns Mooshak.
  • Bird's Eye: Super high zoomed-out perspective.

GAMEPLAY

Explore 4 distinct biomes (North, East, South, West) to recover the 4 lost Blessings. Beware of Banasura's corruption traps, deadly parkour over the East Waters, and falling boulders in the West Mountain! Once you have all 4 blessings, climb the mountain to recover the Ananta Jyoti, then return to the center shrine to summon Ganapati Ji!

WHAT IS THE GAME ABOUT?

In the beginning, the world was illuminated by the wisdom and prosperity of Ganapati Ji. But darkness rose in the form of the thousand-armed demon, Banasura.

Banasura crushed the cities of light one by one, spreading his corruption until only the final Sacred Temple stood. In a devastating attack, Banasura shattered the temple's power, scattering the Four Blessings (Devotion, Prosperity, Wisdom, and Courage) and extinguishing the eternal flame, the Ananta Jyoti.

With Ganapati Ji's physical form banished, it falls to his most loyal and courageous companion, Mooshak (the divine mouse), to save the world.

You play as Mooshak. You must brave the ruined lands, face Banasura's treacherous traps and taunts, and recover the 4 Blessings to reignite the Ananta Jyoti and summon Ganapati Ji back to the world!
